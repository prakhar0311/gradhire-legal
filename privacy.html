GradHire Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

GradHire ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have when you use the GradHire mobile application ("the app").

1. Using GradHire With or Without an Account

You can use GradHire as a guest or sign in with an account.

  • Guest mode: you can upload a resume, view matched jobs, and optimize your resume without an account. Guest data is kept only for your current session and is not saved to an account. It is cleared when you close the app. To provide these features, your resume is still sent to our servers and AI provider for processing (see Section 3).
  • Signed-in users: if you sign in with Apple or Google, your resumes, saved jobs, and application tracker are stored with your account so you can access them later.

2. Information We Collect

Account information

  • When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name, email address, and a unique account identifier from that provider. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email" option, we receive a private relay email address instead of your real one.
  • We do not receive or store your Apple or Google password.

Content you provide

  • Resumes you upload (the PDF file and the text and details extracted from it)
  • Optimized and tailored versions of your resume that you create in the app
  • Skills you confirm you have when improving your resume
  • Jobs you save, your application statuses (such as Applied or Interviewing), and notes you add

Usage and diagnostic information

  • How you use the app, such as which screens you view and which features you use (for example, uploading a resume, viewing jobs, or tapping Apply). We do not include your resume content, name, email, search text, or job descriptions in this usage data.
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data, such as device model, operating system version, and app version
  • An app instance identifier and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address by our analytics provider. We do not collect your precise location.
  • Your IP address, which our servers process to deliver the service and prevent abuse

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Analyze your resume and match it with relevant job listings
  • Generate AI-powered resume optimization suggestions, readiness scores, and improved resume files
  • Save your resumes, saved jobs, and application tracker to your account
  • Keep you signed in and secure your account
  • Fix crashes and bugs, and improve the app's performance and features
  • Prevent abuse, such as excessive or automated requests
  • Respond to your questions and support requests

4. AI Processing

To analyze and optimize resumes, the relevant resume content and job description are sent to our AI provider, OpenAI, through its API. Under OpenAI's API data policies, data sent through the API is not used to train their models by default.

AI-generated suggestions can contain mistakes. GradHire is designed to rewrite only your own content and not to invent skills, experience, or results, but you should always review your resume before using it.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following service providers to operate GradHire. They process data only as needed to provide their services to us:

  • Supabase: account authentication, database, and secure file storage for your resumes and app data
  • OpenAI: AI processing for resume analysis and optimization
  • Render: hosting for our backend servers
  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics): app usage analytics and crash reporting
  • Apple and Google: Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google
  • Adzuna: job listings. We send job search criteria (such as keywords) to Adzuna to retrieve listings; we do not send your resume or personal information to Adzuna

When you tap Apply on a job, the job posting opens on the employer's or job site's website. Any information you share there is governed by that website's own privacy policy.

6. What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not show ads or use your data for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not collect your device's advertising identifier.

7. Data Storage and Retention

  • Account data, resumes, and application tracker data are stored securely with our database provider. Resume files are stored in private storage that only your account can access.
  • We keep your account data until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Some of our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States.
  • Usage and crash data are kept by our analytics provider according to its retention settings.
  • Deleted data may remain in secure backups for a limited period before it is permanently removed.

8. Deleting Your Data and Account

  • You can delete individual resumes and saved jobs at any time in the app.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app under Account > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, resumes, resume files, saved jobs, application tracker, and notes. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke GradHire's access to your Apple ID.
  • If you have a subscription, deleting your account does not cancel it. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.
  • You can also request deletion by emailing us at the address below.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw your consent. Most of this can be done directly in the app. For anything else, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections, access controls so users can only access their own data, and private file storage. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

11. Children's Privacy

GradHire is intended for students, graduates, and job seekers aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If the changes are significant, we may also notify you in the app.
