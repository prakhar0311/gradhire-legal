GradHire Support

Need help using GradHire?

GradHire helps freshers and new grads find entry-level jobs that match their resume, create ATS-friendly resumes tailored to each job, and track their applications.

If you're experiencing issues or have questions about the app, feel free to contact us.

Common Support Topics

  • Signing in with Apple or Google
  • Resume upload issues
  • Job match results and job search
  • AI resume optimization and readiness score
  • Saved resumes and the application tracker
  • Download or file-sharing problems
  • Deleting your account and data
  • General feedback or bug reports

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account and all your data at any time in the app under Account > Delete Account. You can also email us to request deletion.
